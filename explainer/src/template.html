04One round

Count the steps that need a model.

1Split the links into weeks
2Write the briefs
3Score them against your labels
4Report the worst misses
5Propose one change, with a reason
6Check it stayed inside the walls
7Run steps 2 and 3 again
8Keep it if the score rose, else revert
a model: 2 steps
plain code: everything else
step 9: open the hidden week, once
kept reverted by git
