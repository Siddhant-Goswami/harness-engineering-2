The question for today

The loop already rewrites its prompts against your verifier.
What stops it from rewriting the harness too?

One

A verifier the loop cannot game.

Two

A decision about which parts it is allowed to touch.

TIME: minute 3 to 5. Read the question out loud and stop. Take two or three answers from the room before clicking. Expect: “it would break things”, “it would cheat”, “I would not trust it”. Write the answers down; each one maps to one of the two reveals. CLICK 1: a verifier the loop cannot game. “It would cheat” lives here. If the loop can see everything it is graded on, it will learn the grade and not the job. CLICK 2: a decision about what it may touch. “It would break things” lives here. Instructions, sources, skills: yes. Its own permissions, the scoring, the labels: no. Say plainly: the whole 90 minutes is these two things. Everything we build is either part of the verifier or part of the fence.
