TIME: minute 12 to 20. Eight of these minutes are the room working. ASK FIRST: “A brief cannot be graded today. When can it be?” Let someone say “later, when you know what mattered”. Then show the three labels. The three labels, in plain words: - acted on it: you did something because of this link. - should have: you ignored it, and you now wish you had not. - noise: it changed nothing for you. Tell them to label honestly, not kindly. Most saved links are noise, and that is fine. In the sample month, 16 of 25 links a week are noise. While they label, walk the file on screen: date, source, title, url, label. A row with no label is still shown to the agent, it is just never scored. Then run prepare: it splits the month into four weeks and hands the agent a copy WITHOUT the labels. Anyone without their own links uses the sample that ships with the repo; it is already labelled. CLICK when most are done: “Named: the verifier”. This is the callback to 101: write the verifier, not the prompt. The difference today is that the verifier is your own hindsight, and it is written before any agent runs.
